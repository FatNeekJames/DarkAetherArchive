# Dark Aether Archive — project notes

## Black Ops 7 Relics

Relics should become their own Black Ops 7-only collection layer. Do not mix them into ordinary Intel or expose the feature for earlier games.

Future scope:

- Add a Relics tab to each main Black Ops 7 map dossier.
- Track the relic name, unlock requirement, gameplay modifier/effect, completion state, and a verified source.
- Keep Relics separate from Audio Logs, Documents, Transmissions, and Artifacts.
- Cover Ashes of the Damned, Astra Malorum, Paradox Junction, Totenreich, Kowakujō, and Rex Infernus.
- Use The Korborium’s map-specific relic explainers as research leads, then corroborate requirements before publication.
