# Black Ops Cold War Zombies

**Era:** Dark Aether story — Requiem versus Omega, 1983–1985

Cold War begins in the single universe created after Tag der Toten. Most people have no memory of the old multiverse, but Samantha Maxis and Edward “Eddie” Richtofen survived into the new reality. Nazi Projekt Endstation opens a dimensional breach in 1945; Soviet reactivation in 1983 starts an Aetherium arms race between the CIA-backed Requiem and the Soviet Omega Group. **[Confirmed]**[^1][^2]

Cold War replaces sparse map radios with a formal Intel system. Audio logs, documents, radio transmissions and Dark Aether artifacts are grouped by Requiem, Omega, Maxis and Dark Aether perspectives. Intel often unlocks across seasons, so “map launch evidence” and later additions are not always identical sets. **[Confirmed]**[^1]

## Die Maschine

### Easter Egg

- **[Confirmed]** “Seal the Deal/Evil Unleashed” sends a Requiem strike team to close Projekt Endstation's breach. The crew restores the cyclotron, enters the Dark Aether, recovers Pack-a-Punch and assembles the Aetherscope.[^3]
- **[Confirmed]** The summarized route gives Vogel's diary to three spectral reflections, unlocks Der Wechsler, builds the D.I.E. Shockwave and its four elemental variants, captures split Megaton halves in the chamber, restores Orlov's memory, and protects him while he overloads the accelerator. The team then crosses the collapsing site to Raptor One's exfil.[^3]

### Cutscenes

- **[Confirmed]** The intro has Samantha send Weaver Soviet footage of soldiers entering the abandoned Endstation bunker and being attacked, prompting Requiem's deployment on 13 November 1983.[^1]
- **[Confirmed]** The ending shows the strike team escaping as Orlov destroys the facility. Omega observer Kravchenko learns that Requiem is now active in the same war.

### Intel

- **[Confirmed]** Endstation documents begin with the August 1943 cyclotron approval, describe the spacetime breach, D.I.E. weapon and Der Wechsler, and preserve Vogel's final research.[^4]
- **[Confirmed]** Requiem logs introduce Weaver, Grey, Strauss and Carver as department leads. Omega material establishes Operatsiya Grobovshchik and Soviet extraction of Aetherium; Dark Aether artifacts show that objects and people from lost realities persist in the realm.
- **[Confirmed]** Zykov's letter records the Soviet soldier sent inside to deactivate Endstation in 1945, creating the human thread that eventually becomes the Forsaken.

### Ciphers

- **[Confirmed]** Vogel Cipher #1 is an Enigma M3 message with published rotor, ring, position and plugboard settings; its plaintext reports Endstation ready to receive the Reich's “package.” Cipher #2 is ADFGX keyed **HEIMAT**.[^4]
- **[Confirmed]** These are collectible documents whose decrypted versions unlock in the Intel menu, not steps required to close the breach.

### Characters

- **[Confirmed]** The strike team can consist of operators rather than a fixed four-person cast. Weaver commands; Grey, Strauss and Carver advise; Samantha supplies the warning. Vogel and Orlov appear through spectral memory, and Kravchenko represents Omega.

### Confirmed Lore

- **[Confirmed]** Projekt Endstation's accelerator opened the first known breach of this universe, and Omega's reactivation made the local disaster global.[^1][^4]
- **[Confirmed]** Orlov was an Omega subject transformed and memory-wiped by Der Wechsler; reclaiming his identity enables the sacrifice that closes the site.

### Theories

- **[Theory]** Nacht's ruined bunker is used as the surface of the map, but this does not make the original World at War match a remembered historical event in the new universe.

## Firebase Z

### Easter Egg

- **[Confirmed]** “Maxis Potential” sends Requiem to Outpost 25 to rescue Samantha after Peck throws her into the Dark Aether. The crew restores three Aether Reactors, meets Ravenov and confronts Peck.[^1][^5]
- **[Confirmed]** The summarized route creates a truth serum from Peck's memories, delivers it through the ventilation system, captures Mimic memory essences, obtains the correct data, aligns the satellite and opens a portal. Samantha returns carrying objects associated with prior realities, and the strike team defeats an Orda during escape.[^5]

### Cutscenes

- **[Confirmed]** The intro establishes Ravenov as Samantha's Omega contact and Peck as her captor at the Vietnam facility on 15 June 1984.[^1]
- **[Confirmed]** The ending shows Samantha emerge in altered clothes with unusual power; she, Ravenov and the strike team escape after the Orda falls. Peck survives and later remains valuable to Omega.

### Intel

- **[Confirmed]** The map's 91 catalogued entries are divided among Requiem, Omega, Maxis and Dark Aether; 76 launched with the map and 15 arrived in Season Two.[^6]
- **[Confirmed]** Maxis logs cover her relationship with Ravenov, interrogation by Peck and prolonged isolation. Omega documents detail Outpost 25's weapons work; Requiem staff debate trust, rescue priorities and the growing intelligence gap.[^6]
- **[Confirmed]** Dark Aether artifacts and reports show that exposure alters body, memory and perception, foreshadowing Samantha's powers and Zykov's much longer transformation.

### Ciphers

- **[Confirmed]** Omega ciphers and safe combinations hide operational records; Mimic memory extraction is the main quest's decoding mechanic. The recovered “correct” memory set is verified through the computer rather than fan interpretation.

### Characters

- **[Confirmed]** Samantha is the rescue target; Ravenov is the inside agent; Peck leads the research and antagonizes Requiem. Weaver, Grey, Strauss and Carver supervise the operator strike team.

### Confirmed Lore

- **[Confirmed]** Time passes differently in the Dark Aether, and Samantha returns with both new abilities and memories/objects connected to the old Aether story.
- **[Confirmed]** Omega has industrialized Aetherium extraction and creature experimentation beyond Requiem's capabilities.[^1][^6]

### Theories

- **[Theory]** Samantha's recovered items evoke her old life, but Cold War does not initially state that she consciously remembers every Aether-timeline event.

## Outbreak — Operation Threshold interlude

### Easter Egg

- **[Confirmed]** Outbreak bridges Firebase Z and Mauer with two main quests across the Ural regions. “Ravenov Implications” contacts Samantha and Ravenov, enters the Ruka silo, redirects Omega's Reality Inversion warheads and defeats Legion.[^1][^7]
- **[Confirmed]** “Entrapment/Operation Excision” locates the defecting Omega Eight, escorts and extracts them, and ends with Kravchenko capturing the strike team and Raptor One.[^7][^8]
- **[Confirmed]** The optional Pact records prior main-quest completions and grants a permanent starting upgrade; it is progression acknowledgement rather than a separate story ending.

### Cutscenes

- **[Confirmed]** The first quest ends with the missiles diverted into the ocean, frustrating Omega's plan to invert and weaponize huge Outbreak Zones.
- **[Confirmed]** The second ends in betrayal/capture: the supposed extraction is compromised, and Requiem's agents become Omega prisoners before Mauer.[^8]

### Intel

- **[Confirmed]** Outbreak's seasonal archive covers the Omega–Requiem field race, Aetherium ecology, Dark Aether Elder Gods, Ravenov, Peck, Kravchenko, Valentina and Zykov over June–November 1984.[^1][^9]
- **[Confirmed]** Zykov's diaries span thousands of subjective days and report a newer predator consuming rival Elder Gods, evidence for how a stranded soldier becomes a dominant entity.[^9]

### Ciphers

- **[Confirmed]** Seasonal documents include the R Cipher and other Omega correspondence. Radio tuning, microfilm slides, launch-key recovery and silo terminal sequences form the main quests' operational puzzles.[^9]

### Characters

- **[Confirmed]** The strike team is playable; Ravenov and Samantha assist remotely; Kravchenko, Peck and Valentina compete within Omega; the Omega Eight are defectors; Legion is the first quest boss.

### Confirmed Lore

- **[Confirmed]** Outbreak is not a non-story side mode: Treyarch's own recap calls it the bridge from Firebase Z to Mauer and identifies two full main quests.[^1]

### Theories

- **[Theory]** Free exploration permits regions in variable gameplay order. Only dated intel and quest-gated outcomes should be forced into a strict chronology.

## Mauer der Toten

### Easter Egg

- **[Confirmed]** “Tin Man Heart” forces the captured Requiem team to work for Kravchenko in Berlin on 2 February 1985. The objective is to stop Valentina opening a stable invasion route for the army she believes belongs to her father.[^10][^11]
- **[Confirmed]** The summarized route restores power, activates/upgrades Klaus, obtains the CRBR-S, opens Endstation's hidden Berlin lab, constructs and charges an Aetherium Neutralizer/Inversion Warhead, and defeats Valentina through several moving phases.[^10]
- **[Confirmed]** Klaus carries the warhead into the portal and detonates it, closing the breach. Samantha opens an escape portal when Kravchenko then tries to kill the strike team.[^10]

### Cutscenes

- **[Confirmed]** The intro shows the prisoners dropped into divided Berlin under Omega coercion, with Raptor One held as leverage.
- **[Confirmed]** The ending gives Klaus a conscious farewell, demonstrates Samantha's ability to tear open a portal, and leaves Kravchenko injured but alive.

### Intel

- **[Confirmed]** Forty-eight catalogued entries cover Requiem, Omega and Dark Aether sources. They identify Valentina as Angelika Vogel, daughter of Endstation director Ulrich Vogel, and reconstruct her false Soviet identity.[^12]
- **[Confirmed]** Vogel's Operation Baldr documents show Nazi troops deliberately sent into the Dark Aether in January 1945 as a survival army. The “father” guiding Valentina is actually the Forsaken using a trusted shape/voice.[^12]
- **[Confirmed]** Zykov's later journals describe the predator hunting and absorbing Elder Gods, independently reinforcing the Forsaken's rise.[^12]

### Ciphers

- **[Confirmed]** The F Cipher is ADFGX with a recorded key/alphabet. Its plaintext urges Omega to capture rather than kill the Forsaken and cites Zykov's offer of help.[^12]
- **[Confirmed]** Klaus's UV number hunt and warhead conversion are quest codes, while the Intel cipher supplies optional strategic context.

### Characters

- **[Confirmed]** Valentina/Angelika is the principal antagonist; the Forsaken impersonates Vogel. Klaus becomes an autonomous ally. Kravchenko and Peck command the trapped strike team; Samantha rescues it.

### Confirmed Lore

- **[Confirmed]** Valentina, not a surviving Vogel, engineers Operation Baldr's return under the Forsaken's manipulation.[^12]
- **[Confirmed]** Samantha's powers are now strong enough to open a stable emergency portal from outside the immediate machinery.

### Theories

- **[Theory]** Klaus's exact consciousness—independent person, uploaded mind or emergent AI—is emotionally demonstrated but not exhaustively defined.

## Forsaken

### Easter Egg

- **[Confirmed]** “Pyrrhic Victory” is Operation First Domino at Omega's Zakarpatska test site on 4 June 1985. Requiem and Omega both seek Kazimir Zykov, who reveals himself as the Forsaken once released.[^2][^13]
- **[Confirmed]** The summarized route lifts the site lockdown, obtains the Chrysalax, constructs an Aetherium Neutralizer to cross the phase, destroys the Forsaken's crystalline armour and charges four gun turrets with Samantha's energy.[^13]
- **[Confirmed]** Samantha enters the entity's exposed core, forces it into the containment chamber and seals herself in the Dark Aether. The global breaches collapse, producing victory at enormous personal and political cost.[^13]

### Cutscenes

- **[Confirmed]** The mid-quest reveal explains that roughly forty normal-world years became centuries for Zykov; survival through consuming other powers transformed him into the Forsaken.[^13][^14]
- **[Confirmed]** The ending reveals Requiem's Director as the adult Eddie Richtofen. With Maxis removed and the entity captured for Project Janus, he dissolves Requiem and orders Weaver, Grey, Strauss, Carver, Raptor One and the strike team imprisoned at Blacksite 13. A five-years-later scene shows Peck seeking “old friends” near a Japanese site.[^15]

### Intel

- **[Confirmed]** Thirty-six entries across Requiem, Omega, Maxis and Dark Aether document the final alliance, Zykov's manipulation, the Director's hidden agenda and Project Janus.[^14]
- **[Confirmed]** Forsaken crystal recordings retain both identities: he acknowledges having been Zykov while presenting centuries of survival and conquest as the reason for what he became.[^16]

### Ciphers

- **[Confirmed]** The map emphasizes assembled containment technology and entity memories rather than a new classical-cipher corpus. The puzzle is partly epistemic: all factions accept “Zykov” as an informant until the release makes his identity testable.

### Characters

- **[Confirmed]** Samantha and the Forsaken are the central opposing powers; Ravenov and the operator team assist. Kravchenko and Peck lead Omega operations. Eddie/Richtofen is unmasked as Requiem Director.

### Confirmed Lore

- **[Confirmed]** The Director manipulated both the anti-Forsaken mission and Requiem's removal to secure the entity and clear the way for Project Janus.[^15][^17]
- **[Confirmed]** The arrested department heads and Samantha's Dark Aether imprisonment directly establish Black Ops 6's starting conditions.

### Theories

- **[Theory]** Eddie's survival from Tag confirms continuity, but his motives cannot be reduced to Ultimis or Primis Richtofen's personalities. He grew up in the new universe and possesses a distinct life history.

## Onslaught intel note

- **[Confirmed]** Onslaught contains more than forty additional Intel drops and was explicitly recommended by Treyarch as preparation for Mauer. Its entries cover smaller containment operations and relationships among Requiem/Omega personnel.[^1]
- **[Confirmed]** It is indexed here as a supplemental mode rather than a unique round-based map campaign; individual drops should be stored by season and faction when this research is converted to a database.

## Cold War synthesis

- **[Confirmed]** The conflict escalates from one reopened bunker to worldwide zones, but the human rivalry remains exploitable: both superpowers repeatedly convert unknown phenomena into weapons before understanding them.
- **[Confirmed]** Zykov is the season's slow reveal. His letters begin as a sacrifice narrative, his journals chart centuries of adaptation, and his apparent assistance positions him for release.
- **[Confirmed]** Samantha wins the immediate battle and loses her freedom; Requiem wins the arms race and is dismantled by its own Director. The title “Pyrrhic Victory” is therefore literal.
- **[Theory]** The old Aether multiverse is gone, but artifacts and survivors prove its consequences are not. “Reboot” is a production label, not an in-universe claim that nothing before Cold War happened.
