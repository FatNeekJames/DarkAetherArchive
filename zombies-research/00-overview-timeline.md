# Treyarch Zombies — master story timeline

This document stitches the map chapters into a chronological narrative. It separates three continuities—Aether, Chaos and Dark Aether—because release order is not one continuous calendar. The Aether story contains dimensions, time travel and a repeating cycle; Chaos includes a vision of antiquity; Dark Aether begins after the old multiverse is collapsed and later uses a dimension where time is non-linear. **[Confirmed]**[^1][^2]

The official 2017 *Zombies Chronicles* timeline is the authority for the pre-*Black Ops 4* Aether chronology. Later maps can add, reframe or branch from it; they do not make release order a safe substitute for story order. **[Dev-stated]**[^1][^3]

## How to read this chronology

- **[Confirmed]** “Map date” means the date at the destination, not necessarily the amount of time experienced by the travelling crew.
- **[Confirmed]** A map can occupy more than one narrative position. *Mob of the Dead* repeats until its cycle is broken; *Classified* has a playable Pentagon episode and a later ending scene; *Revelations* completes the cycle that *Blood of the Dead* prevents.
- **[Confirmed]** A location inside the Dark Aether can be a memory copied from another time or universe. Such a location is not dated merely by the period its architecture resembles.
- **[Theory]** Where the archive says “best placement,” the evidence establishes sequence but not an exact Gregorian date.

## Storylines at a glance

| Storyline | Narrative spine | End state in this archive |
|---|---|---|
| Aether | **[Confirmed]** Element 115 experiments create the undead crisis; Ultimis pursues Richtofen's Grand Scheme; Victis powers the polarization devices; Primis repeats a soul-collection cycle until Nikolai breaks it. | **[Confirmed]** *Tag der Toten* collapses the multiverse into the Dark Aether and sends Samantha and Eddie into a new universe. |
| Chaos | **[Confirmed]** The Order pursues Sentinel Artifacts and Prima Materia; Scarlett's party follows Alistair's trail through artifact trials. | **[Confirmed]** *Ancient Evil* ends with Medusa holding the Library and a transformed Scarlett, leaving the original Chaos arc unresolved. |

## Aether story — recommended narrative order

The Aether story is presented here in the order a reader should learn it, not by sorting destination years. That keeps the original mystery intact: first Group 935 and Ultimis, then Richtofen's Grand Scheme, Moon's destruction of Earth, Victis and the Maxis ending of *Buried*, and only then the transition to Primis, the Mob cycle, Black Ops 3 and the broken-cycle Black Ops 4 conclusion. **[Confirmed]**[^1]

### 1. Group 935 and the World at War chain

- **[Confirmed]** Dr. Ludvig Maxis and Dr. Edward Richtofen build Group 935's teleportation and weapons programme around Element 115. Richtofen's secret work with the Illuminati, the Moon Pyramid Device and the Shadowman diverges from Maxis's stated scientific mission.[^1]
- **[Confirmed]** Samantha Maxis and her dog Fluffy are drawn into the experiments. After Richtofen betrays Maxis and Samantha enters the Moon Pyramid Device, Samantha controls the undead and turns them against him.
- **[Confirmed]** *Nacht der Untoten* is the ground-level beginning of the playable outbreak record: soldiers are trapped at an airfield bunker amid the results of 115 experimentation. It contains no completed cinematic quest, so later radios and timeline documents supply most of its placement.[01-world-at-war.md](01-world-at-war.md#nacht-der-untoten)
- **[Confirmed]** *Verrückt* records the failed extraction of Peter McCain from Wittenau Sanatorium. Dempsey is captured, the Marines are destroyed and Richtofen's future test subjects are drawn into Group 935's orbit.[01-world-at-war.md](01-world-at-war.md#verrückt)
- **[Confirmed]** At *Shi No Numa*, Ultimis—Richtofen, Dempsey, Takeo and Nikolai—forms around Division 9's Rising Sun facility. Peter's hanging body, the 115 meteor and Richtofen's messages turn the four survivors into a directed expedition.[01-world-at-war.md](01-world-at-war.md#shi-no-numa)
- **[Confirmed]** On 28 October 1945, Ultimis returns to *Der Riese*. Teleporters, Pack-a-Punch and Maxis's recorded betrayal expose Richtofen's hand; an overload sends the crew forward rather than ending the outbreak.[01-world-at-war.md](01-world-at-war.md#der-riese)

### 2. Ultimis and Richtofen's Grand Scheme

- **[Confirmed]** *Kino der Toten* occurs at a Berlin Group 935 theatre on 28 October 1963. Ultimis arrives from 1945, repairs the teleporter and continues following clues toward the equipment Richtofen needs.[02-black-ops-1.md](02-black-ops-1.md#kino-der-toten)
- **[Confirmed]** *Ascension* brings Ultimis to the Soviet Cosmodrome while the Pentagon cast fights the concurrent *Five* outbreak. The crew frees Gersh from the Casimir mechanism and advances Richtofen's plan.[02-black-ops-1.md](02-black-ops-1.md#ascension)
- **[Confirmed]** At *Call of the Dead*, in Siberia during 2011, Ultimis is trapped behind a sealed door while a film crew confronts George Romero and the undead. The celebrities recover the Golden Rod for Richtofen.[02-black-ops-1.md](02-black-ops-1.md#call-of-the-dead)
- **[Confirmed]** At *Shangri-La*, Brock and Gary are caught in a time loop while Ultimis uses the eclipse mechanism to acquire the Focusing Stone. Its destination date is less important than its firm narrative position: after the Golden Rod and before Moon.[02-black-ops-1.md](02-black-ops-1.md#shangri-la)
- **[Confirmed]** The playable *Classified* Pentagon incident fits into Ultimis's pre-Moon journey, but its ending scene belongs to the later broken-cycle story and is held back until Black Ops 4 below.[^6]

### 3. Moon and Nuketown — Earth is broken

- **[Confirmed]** On 13 October 2025, Ultimis reaches Griffin Station in *Moon*. Richtofen combines the Golden Rod and Focusing Stone, exchanges bodies with Samantha inside the MPD and gains control of the undead.[^1]
- **[Confirmed]** Maxis convinces the remaining crew to launch missiles at Earth. The strike fractures rather than saves the planet; Richtofen remains in the Aether and Samantha occupies Richtofen's body.[02-black-ops-1.md](02-black-ops-1.md#moon)
- **[Confirmed]** *Nuketown Zombies* unfolds at the Nevada site during the Moon catastrophe. Richtofen's and Samantha's eye-colour/control transition and the arriving missile make the concurrence observable in play.[03-black-ops-2.md](03-black-ops-2.md#tranzit--green-run)

### 4. Victis and the Maxis ending of Buried

- **[Confirmed]** In *TranZit/Green Run*, Samuel Stuhlinger, Abigail “Misty” Briarton, Marlton Johnson and Russman—Victis—cross the devastated Earth while Maxis and Richtofen issue incompatible instructions for the polarization towers.[03-black-ops-2.md](03-black-ops-2.md#tranzit--green-run)
- **[Confirmed]** In *Die Rise*, Victis is displaced to a shattered Shanghai skyscraper complex and activates a second tower. Richtofen alone can speak directly through Stuhlinger because Stuhlinger consumed zombie flesh.[03-black-ops-2.md](03-black-ops-2.md#die-rise)
- **[Confirmed]** On 31 December 2035, *Buried* takes Victis to an underground Western town in Angola. Completing the Maxis side of the three-map super Easter egg gives the branch continued by later Aether material: Maxis gains control, displaces Richtofen into a zombie and begins destroying the remaining Earth while trying to reach Samantha.[^7]
- **[Confirmed]** The Richtofen super Easter egg is also playable, but later Aether chapters do not continue from his victory. It remains an alternate branch rather than the transition used for the main archive narrative.
- **[Confirmed]** The story now transitions to *Origins*. This is a narrative handoff into another dimension and crew, not Victis physically travelling from 2035 to 1918.

### 5. Origins — the Primis story begins

- **[Confirmed]** In Dimension 63 on 4 June 1918, younger alternate versions of Richtofen, Dempsey, Takeo and Nikolai converge at Excavation Site 64 and become the crew called Primis.[^4]
- **[Confirmed]** In *Origins*, Primis builds and upgrades the four elemental staffs, releases Samantha from Agartha and learns that their task extends beyond the battlefield. The ending's House imagery is a real story layer, not evidence that the preceding maps were only a child's fiction.[03-black-ops-2.md](03-black-ops-2.md#origins)
- **[Confirmed]** Primis Richtofen's access to the Kronorium and his blood-vial contingencies leads into the soul-collection mission followed in Black Ops 3.

### 6. Mob of the Dead — the cycle's lock and key

- **[Confirmed]** After introducing Primis through *Origins*, the archive next explains *Mob of the Dead* because Alcatraz determines whether Primis's later route repeats or breaks. The remembered event itself occurs on New Year's Eve 1933.[^5]
- **[Confirmed]** Billy Handsome, Sal DeLuca, Finn O'Leary and Albert “Weasel” Arlington repeatedly die and return in purgatorial Alcatraz. Their failed escape on Icarus is reenacted each cycle.[03-black-ops-2.md](03-black-ops-2.md#mob-of-the-dead)
- **[Confirmed]** If the three mobsters kill Weasel, the cycle continues. If Weasel kills them, the cycle is broken. The broken state later changes the Kronorium and diverts Primis into *Blood of the Dead*.
- **[Confirmed]** Because the prison is a pocket outside ordinary causality, 1933 identifies the murder and failed escape being repeated; it does not mean the Primis story should be read before World at War.

### 7. Black Ops 3 — the repeating Primis cycle

- **[Confirmed]** *Shadows of Evil* supplies the Summoning Key in Morg City, where the Shadowman tricks Nero, Jessica, Jack and Floyd into opening his route. Primis Richtofen takes the Key and leaves the Shadows crew behind.[04-black-ops-3.md](04-black-ops-3.md#shadows-of-evil)
- **[Confirmed]** In *The Giant*, Primis confronts an older Richtofen at Der Riese. Young Richtofen kills him and the group begins collecting the souls of their alternate selves.[04-black-ops-3.md](04-black-ops-3.md#the-giant)
- **[Confirmed]** *Der Eisendrache* targets Ultimis Dempsey. Primis destroys the Moon installation in that dimension and captures Dempsey's soul after his death.[04-black-ops-3.md](04-black-ops-3.md#der-eisendrache)
- **[Confirmed]** *Zetsubou No Shima* targets Takeo. The dying older Takeo accepts the Emperor's betrayal and permits Primis Takeo to take his soul; Richtofen also secures the Alcatraz blood vials.[04-black-ops-3.md](04-black-ops-3.md#zetsubou-no-shima)
- **[Confirmed]** In the cycle-continuing route, Primis travels to *Gorod Krovi*, kills the Ultimis Nikolai whose soul has not been preserved and completes Richtofen's collection.[04-black-ops-3.md](04-black-ops-3.md#gorod-krovi)
- **[Confirmed]** In *Revelations*, the Shadowman invades Monty's perfect world. Primis imprisons him in the Summoning Key, but the crew's blood makes them paradoxes Monty cannot simply erase.[04-black-ops-3.md](04-black-ops-3.md#revelations)
- **[Confirmed]** Monty sends Primis into the ancient Great War, where they become the legendary warriors whose history feeds back toward *Origins*. The Keepers, Apothicons, Monty and Shadowman form the ancient cosmology behind this repeating loop.[^3]

### 8. Black Ops 4 — the cycle breaks

- **[Confirmed]** Black Ops 4 follows the version of events in which Weasel broke the Mob cycle. During Primis's post-Zetsubou Alcatraz stop in *Blood of the Dead*, the Kronorium changes and the route to *Gorod Krovi* and *Revelations* is interrupted.[05-black-ops-4.md](05-black-ops-4.md#blood-of-the-dead)
- **[Confirmed]** The Warden/Brutus uses the Dark Mechanism to drain Primis Richtofen's blood. A post-*Revelations* Richtofen emerges from cryostasis, destroys the prison's controlling structure and leaves the dying Richtofen behind.
- **[Confirmed]** Nikolai receives the Kronorium and assumes leadership because his soul was never placed in the House. That difference makes him able to carry out the final plan.
- **[Confirmed]** The *Classified* ending now becomes relevant: Primis reaches Groom Lake and takes the post-Moon Ultimis crew. This ending is later than the playable Pentagon incident.[05-black-ops-4.md](05-black-ops-4.md#classified)
- **[Confirmed]** In *Alpha Omega*, Primis and Ultimis enter Camp Edward/Nuketown together. Rushmore, Broken Arrow, the Avogadro and the Elemental Shard connect the ruined-Earth programme to earlier 115 research.[05-black-ops-4.md](05-black-ops-4.md#alpha-omega)
- **[Confirmed]** Samantha and Eddie leave the American Pyramid Device. The adult Maxis is killed by Monty, and Samantha commits herself to fighting him.
- **[Confirmed]** In *Tag der Toten*, Nikolai sends Victis to the Siberian facility to assemble the Agarthan Device. Pablo escapes toward the Great War, completing the exchange that allowed post-*Revelations* Richtofen to reach Alcatraz.[05-black-ops-4.md](05-black-ops-4.md#tag-der-toten)
- **[Confirmed]** Nikolai destroys the Summoning Key and poisons Primis and Ultimis; Samantha kills Nikolai at his request. The multiverse, its 115 corruption and its paradox-bound inhabitants are consigned to the Dark Aether.
- **[Confirmed]** Child Samantha and Eddie walk into the new single universe. This ends the Aether story and creates the starting condition for the Dark Aether saga.[^8]

## Chaos story — its own chronology

### Ancient background

- **[Confirmed]** A primordial material called Prima Materia and four Sentinel Artifacts associated with Danu, Ra, Zeus and Odin underlie the Chaos trials. The Order seeks these objects and the Library's knowledge across centuries.[^9]
- **[Confirmed]** Perseus reaches Delphi in antiquity, confronts the Oracle and is deceived by Medusa. These events survive through scrolls and the petrified city rather than as a separately playable chronological map.[05-black-ops-4.md](05-black-ops-4.md#ancient-evil)

### 20 March 1912 — Dead of the Night

- **[Confirmed]** Alistair Rhodes gathers guests at his English estate. The Order attacks, kidnaps Alistair and uses Prima Materia to transform the party; Godfrey, under the Order's influence, kills the other three playable guests after the Werewolf is defeated.[^10]
- **[Confirmed]** *Dead of the Night* is chronologically first although it released after *Voyage* and *IX*. The date is displayed in its intro.[05-black-ops-4.md](05-black-ops-4.md#dead-of-the-night)

### 14–15 April 1912 — Voyage of Despair

- **[Confirmed]** Scarlett Rhodes, Diego Necalli, Bruno Delacroix and Stanton Shaw board RMS Titanic to steal the Odin Sentinel Artifact. An Order cultist activates it, releases Prima Materia and turns passengers and crew as the ship strikes the iceberg.[^11]
- **[Confirmed]** Completing the Artifact's trial defeats the Eye of Odin and reverses the passengers' transformation, but the historical sinking continues. Shaw identifies Delphi as the route to Alistair.[05-black-ops-4.md](05-black-ops-4.md#voyage-of-despair)

### After Voyage — IX as an induced vision

- **[Confirmed]** At Delphi, the crew inhales vapour and experiences *IX*, a gladiatorial trial in an ancient arena. The apparent ancient date describes the vision's setting; the crew experiences it after Titanic in 1912.[05-black-ops-4.md](05-black-ops-4.md#ix)
- **[Confirmed]** The trial reveals the High Priest, the Order and the four god-aligned systems. Returning from the vision leaves the party ready to enter the real Delphi below.

### After 15 April 1912 — Ancient Evil

- **[Confirmed]** Scarlett's group descends into the hidden Delphi of *Ancient Evil*. Scarlett and Diego cooperate across separated city levels, free the Eagles and defeat Perseus, who is revealed as a transformed victim rather than the ultimate antagonist.[^12]
- **[Confirmed]** The Oracle is Medusa. She takes control of Scarlett and declares her pursuit of the Library; Bruno, already compromised by the Order, attacks Shaw. No released BO4 Chaos map resolves this cliffhanger.
- **[Theory]** Later Dark Aether references to Alistair Rhodes, Sentinel Artifacts and Prima Materia establish counterparts or inherited concepts in the new universe. They do not retroactively confirm that every Chaos event occurred unchanged on the Dark Aether universe's calendar.

## Dark Aether story — chronological narrative

### Creation of the new universe and the sealed past

- **[Confirmed]** After *Tag der Toten*, the previous multiverse is contained in the Dark Aether and a single material universe continues without the old cycle. Samantha and Eddie grow up there, carrying history that most inhabitants cannot know.[^8]
- **[Confirmed]** During the Second World War, Projekt Endstation opens a dimensional breach. Soviet soldier Kazimir Zykov enters to close it and remains trapped, while the site is buried and suppressed.[^13]
- **[Confirmed]** Zykov survives by learning and exploiting the Dark Aether, eventually absorbing entities and becoming the Forsaken. His apparent role as a helpful stranded human remains useful to his escape plan.

### 13 November 1983 — Die Maschine

- **[Confirmed]** Omega Group reactivates Endstation; Samantha anonymously warns Weaver, and the newly formed Requiem sends a strike team to Morasko, Poland.[^14]
- **[Confirmed]** In *Die Maschine*, the team reconstructs the Aetherscope, helps Orlov understand his transformation and overloads the cyclotron. Orlov destroys the facility while the strike team escapes.[06-cold-war.md](06-cold-war.md#die-maschine)
- **[Confirmed]** The operation establishes the basic Cold War conflict: Requiem wants containment but also weapon research, Omega wants strategic superiority, and both institutions extract Aetherium from a realm they only partly understand.

### 15 June 1984 — Firebase Z

- **[Confirmed]** At Omega Outpost 25 in Vietnam, Ravenov helps Requiem enter the base and retrieve Samantha from the Dark Aether. Doctor Peck has tortured and manipulated personnel while developing portal and Aetherium systems.[^15]
- **[Confirmed]** In *Firebase Z*, the strike team stabilizes reactors, identifies transformed defectors through Mimic memories, opens the portal and defeats Orda. Samantha returns with growing powers and an apparent connection to objects from the old Aether story.[06-cold-war.md](06-cold-war.md#firebase-z)

### June 1984–March 1985 — Outbreak / Operation Threshold

- **[Confirmed]** Operation Threshold begins on 20 June 1984 across the Ural Outbreak Zone. Requiem investigates regions in a gameplay-variable order, while dated Intel and quest gates establish the operational sequence.[06-cold-war.md](06-cold-war.md#outbreak--operation-threshold-interlude)
- **[Confirmed]** At Ruka, the strike team prevents Omega from using Reality Inversion warheads to open global breaches. Maxis's unauthorized help deepens Richtofen's interest in controlling her.
- **[Confirmed]** During Operation Excision, Requiem attempts to extract Omega defectors but is captured by Kravchenko. This leads directly to the forced Berlin mission.

### 2 February 1985 — Mauer der Toten

- **[Confirmed]** In *Mauer der Toten*, Kravchenko forces the captured strike team to stop Valentina in East Berlin. Klaus and an inversion warhead close the breach after Valentina is defeated; Samantha opens the escape portal.[^16]
- **[Confirmed]** Valentina is acting through the identity and programme planted by Endstation's legacy. Her defeat removes one route into the Dark Aether but does not stop the Forsaken's larger manipulation.[06-cold-war.md](06-cold-war.md#mauer-der-toten)

### 4 June 1985 — Forsaken

- **[Confirmed]** Requiem enters Test Site Anna to secure Zykov before Omega. Zykov reveals himself as the Forsaken and attempts to cross into the material world.[^17]
- **[Confirmed]** Samantha enters the Dark Aether and contains the Forsaken while the strike team seals them inside Peck's device. Director Richtofen arrests Requiem's senior staff and closes the organization.[06-cold-war.md](06-cold-war.md#forsaken)
- **[Confirmed]** Peck survives and later seeks the imprisoned team at Terminus. Richtofen converts the captured Forsaken, Samantha-related research and his personal losses into Project Janus.

### Continuity bridge — 1944 and the 2020s

- **[Confirmed]** *Vanguard* material supplies earlier Dark Aether entities and 1944 occult history; Operation Deadbolt/*Modern Warfare Zombies* supplies the later deaths of Weaver, Grey, Carver and Strauss, Ava Jansen's identity and the first explicit Shadowsmith warning.[^18]
- **[Confirmed]** Those games are not expanded into map chapters here because the requested per-map scope names World at War through BO7's listed Treyarch round-based releases. Their confirmed consequences are included only where BO6/BO7 require them.
- **[Confirmed]** Operation Deadbolt occurs later on the material-world calendar than BO6, yet BO7's Dark Aether events can involve Ava before Ex-Requiem has returned to perform the 1993 actions that create her. That is a causal loop enabled by the dimension's non-linear time.

### 18 February 1991 — Terminus and Liberty Falls

- **[Confirmed]** Peck and Maya free Weaver, Grey, Carver and Strauss at Terminus while a simultaneous Project Janus breach consumes Liberty Falls.[^19]
- **[Confirmed]** In *Terminus*, the crew kills the transformed Nathan Aguinaldo, defeats Patient 13/Owen Guthrie and escapes Modi's prison operation. Krafft and the Sentinel Artifact become their next lead.[07-black-ops-6.md](07-black-ops-6.md#terminus)
- **[Confirmed]** In *Liberty Falls*, Panos directs a Janus response team to charge the S.D.G., then escapes by exchanging places with them. Richtofen disappears during the same disaster and the town's local cult/Aetherella history is exposed.[07-black-ops-6.md](07-black-ops-6.md#liberty-falls)

### Late February 1991 — the Avalon Artifact route

- **[Confirmed]** At *Citadelle des Morts*, the crew wields four elemental swords, defeats the Guardian and reaches Gabriel Krafft. Mortally wounded, Krafft identifies the excavation containing the Sentinel Artifact.[07-black-ops-6.md](07-black-ops-6.md#citadelle-des-morts)
- **[Confirmed]** At *The Tomb*, the crew rebuilds the Staff of Ice, completes four Artifact trials and identifies the genuine Odin Artifact among false copies. S.A.M. persuades them to deliver it to Sam Colton Hall.[07-black-ops-6.md](07-black-ops-6.md#the-tomb)
- **[Confirmed]** At *Shattered Veil* on 28 February, S.A.M. uses the Artifact and releases the Z-Rex. Richtofen reveals that human Samantha was consumed by the Forsaken and that S.A.M. is using her memories while seeking a body; he wants the Artifact to restore Mary and Samuel.[07-black-ops-6.md](07-black-ops-6.md#shattered-veil)
- **[Confirmed]** The crew and Richtofen form a temporary alliance against S.A.M. and move to Janus Towers.

### 1991 — Reckoning branch and convergence

- **[Confirmed]** In *Reckoning*, players can help Richtofen restore Mary and Samuel or help S.A.M. create a Samantha-shaped body. The rejected party becomes the boss.[07-black-ops-6.md](07-black-ops-6.md#reckoning)
- **[Confirmed]** Both playable choices converge: Weaver, Grey, Carver and Maya enter the Dark Aether and meet alternate Richtofen, Dempsey, Takeo and Nikolai. No released Intel has yet made one reconstruction choice uniquely canonical.

### After Reckoning by crew experience — Black Ops 7

- **[Confirmed]** The Warden drains the eight characters and traps them in the memory geography of *Ashes of the Damned*. They free Veytharion and restore Maya's and Dempsey's stolen soul portions.[08-black-ops-7.md](08-black-ops-7.md#ashes-of-the-damned)
- **[Confirmed]** At *Astra Malorum*, an observatory floats in Saturn's rings. The crew defeats and frees Caltheris, but the Warden captures the travellers and their helper.[08-black-ops-7.md](08-black-ops-7.md#astra-malorum)
- **[Confirmed]** In *Paradox Junction*, the group escapes a Nuketown-derived temporal prison by following the Twins' games and destroying the Dark Heart. The Twins direct them to a cold location.[08-black-ops-7.md](08-black-ops-7.md#paradox-junction)
- **[Confirmed]** At *Totenreich*, the group enters the memory of Eidskallen and a Group 935 giant programme. Tyr helps defeat and free Dravakar, who points them to his sister Nyxara and the World Seed.[08-black-ops-7.md](08-black-ops-7.md#totenreich)
- **[Confirmed]** At *Kowakujō*, the crew solves the murder of alternate Takeo's father, frees Nyxara and claims the World Seed. All four surviving named Shadowsmiths can now oppose the Warden.[08-black-ops-7.md](08-black-ops-7.md#kowakujō)
- **[Confirmed]** In *Rex Infernus*, the group cleanses four Shadowsmith places of power and defeats the Warden. Ex-Requiem returns toward the fate already known from Operation Deadbolt; Ava remains connected to the realm, while alternate Richtofen, Dempsey, Takeo and Nikolai enter a new portal.[08-black-ops-7.md](08-black-ops-7.md#rex-infernus)

### 1993 and the later consequence

- **[Confirmed]** After returning from the Dark Aether, Grey becomes surrogate mother to Ava using genetic samples from Samantha Maxis and Sergei Ravenov. This must happen after BO7 for Ex-Requiem but before Ava's adult Operation Deadbolt life on the material calendar.[^20]
- **[Confirmed]** Weaver, Grey, Carver and Strauss later sacrifice themselves, producing enriched Aetherium vials and leaving the message Ava discovers. Ravenov survives to watch over her.
- **[Theory]** The best current model is a fulfilled causal loop: adult Ava helps events that return her future creators to 1991, then their later actions produce Ava. Whether a future story can alter that loop without contradiction remains open.

## Branch and conflict register

### Confirmed branches

- **[Confirmed]** *Buried*: Maxis and Richtofen outcomes are both playable; the later Aether story continues from Maxis's victory.
- **[Confirmed]** *Mob of the Dead*: cycle continuation and cycle break are both necessary narrative states. The break enables *Blood of the Dead*.
- **[Confirmed]** Primis: the repeating route runs Zetsubou → Gorod Krovi → Revelations → Great War; the broken route runs Zetsubou → Blood of the Dead → Classified ending → Alpha Omega → Tag der Toten.
- **[Confirmed]** *Reckoning*: Richtofen and S.A.M. paths differ in who receives the Artifact's reconstruction and who is fought, then converge in the same BO7 setup.

### Dates that should not be flattened

- **[Confirmed]** *Shangri-La* has a time-looped destination and a clear crew position but contested calendar labelling. Store sequence and destination date separately.
- **[Confirmed]** *Classified* requires two timeline records: Pentagon incident and post-*Blood* ending.
- **[Confirmed]** *IX* requires an experienced date (after Titanic in 1912) and a vision setting (antiquity).
- **[Confirmed]** BO7 maps require a crew-order field and “Dark Aether/non-linear” date state, not invented Earth dates.

### Identity disputes

- **[Confirmed]** S.A.M. has Samantha's memories but is an artificial person with an independent body plan; describing her as simply “Samantha Maxis” erases the central BO6 reveal.
- **[Theory]** The BO7 Twins appear to divide Samantha/Entity-associated memories across living and zombified states. Sources disagree on whether they are one being, two beings or manifestations; preserve the ambiguity.
- **[Confirmed]** BO7's Richtofen, Dempsey, Takeo and Nikolai are alternate incarnations, not silently resurrected Ultimis or Primis.
- **[Theory]** Chaos-to-Dark-Aether links may mean counterparts, absorbed concepts or a reconstituted version of Chaos history. Current evidence confirms the names and artifacts, not a complete unchanged timeline merger.

## Recommended archive ordering

- **[Confirmed]** For learning the Aether plot, present World at War/Ultimis → Richtofen's Grand Scheme → *Moon* and *Nuketown* → Victis and the Maxis ending of *Buried* → *Origins* → *Mob of the Dead* → the Black Ops 3 cycle → the Black Ops 4 broken-cycle conclusion. Keep dates as metadata rather than using them to sort the narrative.
- **[Confirmed]** Present Chaos as Dead of the Night → Voyage of Despair → IX → Ancient Evil, while displaying IX's ancient vision as nested time.
- **[Confirmed]** Present Dark Aether as Die Maschine → Firebase Z → Outbreak operations → Mauer der Toten → Forsaken → Terminus/Liberty Falls → Citadelle → Tomb → Shattered Veil → Reckoning → the six BO7 maps.
- **[Confirmed]** Add optional bridge cards for Vanguard and Operation Deadbolt wherever their evidence becomes necessary, without mixing their maps into the requested Treyarch round-based index.
