# Black Ops 6 Zombies

**Era:** Dark Aether story — Project Janus, the Sentinel Artifact, and the 1991 incursion

Black Ops 6 resumes more than five years after Forsaken. Weaver, Grey, Carver, Strauss and Raptor One have spent that interval in Richtofen's Terminus prison; Maya Aguinaldo and William Peck trigger their escape during simultaneous February 1991 breaches at Terminus and Liberty Falls. The six-map season reunites the survivors, exposes Project Janus and ends with a player choice between Richtofen and the S.A.M. artificial intelligence. **[Confirmed]**[^1][^2]

The game contains 140 collectible Zombies Intel entries across six maps, organized as Audio Logs, Documents and Artifacts. This chapter summarizes their revelations rather than reproducing copyrighted transcripts. **[Confirmed]**[^3]

## Terminus

### Easter Egg

- **[Confirmed]** The main quest restores the island's AMP generators, builds the DRI-11 Beamsmasher, retrieves Modi's hard drive and has S.A.M. decrypt the prison systems. The crew locates Nathan Aguinaldo, now an Amalgam, and is forced to kill him.[^4]
- **[Confirmed]** The summarized latter route obtains the keycard, collects Node Connectors around the island, repairs the network, uses buoys and Modi's terminal to force her submarine back, then defuses three bombs before Patient 13 can weaponize the inversion payload.[^4]
- **[Confirmed]** Patient 13 consumes Modi and the submarine, mutates from the warheads and becomes the final ocean-platform boss. Its defeat secures the escape.

### Cutscenes

- **[Confirmed]** The intro shows Peck and Maya freeing the former Requiem leaders on 18 February 1991 as an outbreak engulfs Terminus. Maya delays departure to find her brother.[^1]
- **[Confirmed]** The ending records Nathan and Modi's deaths, Patient 13's destruction and the group's plan to reach Gabriel Krafft in Avalon for information about the Sentinel Artifact.

### Intel

- **[Confirmed]** Terminus has 30 entries: 16 audio logs, nine documents and five artifacts.[^5]
- **[Confirmed]** Owen Guthrie's recordings chart his guilt, discovery of Modi's abuses and conversion into Patient 13. Other files document Nathan, the Amalgam programme, inversion-warhead recovery, S.A.M.'s construction and Richtofen's personal oversight.[^5][^6]
- **[Confirmed]** The prison records show that Project Janus used its former Requiem personnel and other captives as research subjects, not merely detainees.

### Ciphers

- **[Confirmed]** The Beamsmasher laptop requires three variable numerical results derived from lab devices; the buoy broadcast, Node Connector routing and bomb symbols form the quest's code layer.
- **[Confirmed]** “Code Words” and “Numbers and Faces” are intelligence titles with narrative content; not every numbered document is a cryptographic cipher.

### Characters

- **[Confirmed]** Weaver, Grey, Carver and Maya are the canon playable crew; operators are gameplay alternatives. Peck and Strauss handle comms; Raptor One extracts. Modi, Nathan and Patient 13/Owen Guthrie are the major targets.

### Confirmed Lore

- **[Confirmed]** Patient 13 is Owen Guthrie, punished after attempting to expose Modi; Nathan is another victim of the same experimentation.[^6]
- **[Confirmed]** S.A.M. is a synthetic mind modelled on Samantha, created under Richtofen's programme, and not automatically identical to the human Maxis.

### Theories

- **[Theory]** S.A.M.'s Samantha-like mannerisms invite the assumption that she contains the complete human soul. The season later confirms transferred memories, but not a simple one-to-one identity.

## Liberty Falls

### Easter Egg

- **[Confirmed]** “Bye-Bye, Dark Aether” follows a Project Janus response team at the West Virginia outbreak. Players build the Thrustodyne M23, use it to recover L.T.G. components and assemble the device for Dr. Pericles Panos.[^7]
- **[Confirmed]** The summarized route opens dimensional tears, captures named high-value entities in Aether canisters, uses Strauss counters to calibrate their energy and powers the S.D.G. generator in the church during a final lockdown.[^7]
- **[Confirmed]** Panos uses the accumulated energy to leave the Dark Aether and exchanges places with the strike team, trapping them inside. The successful machine operation is therefore a betrayal, not a clean rescue.

### Cutscenes

- **[Confirmed]** The intro shows Richtofen at Project Janus headquarters as the incursion begins; his family-related objectives and the facility's emergency become the hidden second side of the Terminus breakout.
- **[Confirmed]** The ending reveals Panos's escape and the team lost in darkness, while the larger incident remains active around Liberty Falls.

### Intel

- **[Confirmed]** Liberty Falls has 18 entries. Logs including “Quantum Mechanical Failure,” “The House Josiah Built,” “Steel Mountain Rescue” and “Bear Witness” document failed breach closure, local occult history, emergency response and Janus secrecy.[^8]
- **[Confirmed]** Aetherella comics/statues and the Thrustodyne turn local pop culture and a returning weapon into artifacts with traceable in-world ownership, not merely player jokes.

### Ciphers

- **[Confirmed]** The church machine's Strauss-counter colours and projector readouts require players to translate device feedback into canister settings. The bank-vault combination is assembled from dated locations around town.

### Characters

- **[Confirmed]** Panos directs and betrays the operator team. Richtofen, Blanchard, Moline, Pelletier and local figures are recorded through the intro/intel.

### Confirmed Lore

- **[Confirmed]** Gateway triangulation fails at Project Janus, causing a town-wide breach concurrent with Terminus.[^8]
- **[Confirmed]** Panos returns physically by shifting the strike team into the Dark Aether, explaining the vanished Alpha Team without presenting him as a trustworthy survivor.

### Theories

- **[Theory]** Panos speaks to an unknown entity during quest states. Assigning that voice to a specific later villain before an explicit identification remains speculation.

## Citadelle des Morts

### Easter Egg

- **[Confirmed]** The crew reaches the Avalon castle to rescue Krafft and find the Artifact. Players obtain four Bastard Swords, convert them into elemental weapons, unlock matching incantations and complete four page/trap challenges.[^9][^10]
- **[Confirmed]** The summarized endgame uses the swords and incantations to charge the Mystic Orb at four elemental sites, opens the Owl's secret study, retrieves the Guardian Key and defeats the stone Guardian as lava consumes the arena.[^9]

### Cutscenes

- **[Confirmed]** The intro presents the ex-Requiem expedition entering a French Syndicate fortress after Terminus, with Krafft under interrogation.[^10]
- **[Confirmed]** The ending reveals Krafft mortally wounded after the Guardian fight. He directs the crew to the Avalon dig site and warns that the Obscurus Altilium/Artifact can consume its seekers.
- **[Confirmed]** Maya's optional “Revenge” cutscene confronts and kills Franco for Nathan's fate; it is character-specific but canonical context rather than the main ending.

### Intel

- **[Confirmed]** Logs and scrolls connect Richtofen to Franco's hunt for Krafft, Alistair Rhodes to the earlier excavation, Guy de Saint-Michel's sons to the four swords, and the Owl to the Guardian.[^11]
- **[Confirmed]** The castle's medieval crime history and elemental rites show that the Sentinel network predates Project Janus and Aetherium science.

### Ciphers

- **[Confirmed]** Raven sword symbols, tavern wall markings, alchemical pages, incantation orders and Mystic Orb challenges are repeatable symbolic puzzles. The page sequence is read through environmental trap emblems rather than a prose cipher.

### Characters

- **[Confirmed]** The fixed four remain playable. Krafft supplies the objective; Franco and the Syndicate oppose them; Guy, the four sons and Owl appear through recordings/rites; the Guardian is the boss.

### Confirmed Lore

- **[Confirmed]** Alistair Rhodes previously commissioned the dig that sought the same Sentinel Artifact, explicitly importing Chaos-story material into Dark Aether continuity.[^11]

### Theories

- **[Theory]** This connection does not prove every event of the original Chaos timeline occurred unchanged in the Dark Aether universe; it confirms corresponding people, texts and artifacts.

## The Tomb

### Easter Egg

- **[Confirmed]** The crew carries Krafft's amulet to the Avalon excavation. Players open the Dark Aether Nexus, assemble and upgrade the Staff of Ice, translate its symbols and charge four statues through entity-specific trials.[^12]
- **[Confirmed]** The nine-phase finale distinguishes genuine and false copies of the Odin Sentinel Artifact, destroys artifact-bearing HVTs and interrupts a final wipe attack. The crew then claims the physical Artifact.[^13]

### Cutscenes

- **[Confirmed]** The ending shows the four emerge with the Artifact and board Raptor One's helicopter. S.A.M. reviews Rhodes, Krafft and Shem files and orders delivery to Sam Colton Hall, promising that Samantha can be recovered.[^12]

### Intel

- **[Confirmed]** Archibald Fotherington-Smythe's recordings reconstruct Alistair's expedition and the excavation. Other items cover cult activity, the four known Odin/Danu/Ra/Zeus Artifacts and the relic's Prima Materia function.[^12][^14]
- **[Confirmed]** Shem's record identifies the Liberty Falls mansion/serpent mound as a site chosen by a voice from beyond, connecting the next destination to older local worship.

### Ciphers

- **[Confirmed]** Ice-staff constellations, Roman numerals, portal glyphs and statue weaknesses form the quest's symbolic code. The ending files provide decoded research conclusions rather than an unsolved alphabet ciphertext.

### Characters

- **[Confirmed]** Ex-Requiem and Maya are playable; Archibald and Alistair appear in historical recordings; S.A.M. controls the next mission.

### Confirmed Lore

- **[Confirmed]** The recovered object is the Odin Sentinel Artifact, one of four Artifacts that gather, store and manipulate Prima Materia.[^15]

### Theories

- **[Theory]** S.A.M.'s promise is strategic persuasion. At this point, it is not proof that the human Samantha remains recoverable.

## Shattered Veil

### Easter Egg

- **[Confirmed]** At Sam Colton Hall on 28 February 1991, players obtain the Ray Gun Mark II and its three variants, restore S.A.M.'s mainframe access and perform three Artifact-charging rituals.[^16][^17]
- **[Confirmed]** Placing the Sentinel Artifact before S.A.M. releases the Z-Rex, a Tyrannosaurus skeleton animated and mutated by the site's energy. Defeating it completes the operation but allows S.A.M. to advance her own plan.[^17]

### Cutscenes

- **[Confirmed]** The intro brings the crew to Richtofen's converted mansion while S.A.M. presents herself as the route to Maxis.[^18]
- **[Confirmed]** The ending has Richtofen confront the crew and reveal that the human Samantha was consumed after Forsaken; S.A.M. used Samantha's memories and their grief to obtain the Artifact. Richtofen explains that he intended it to restore his dead wife Mary and son Samuel. The crew forms an uneasy alliance with him and moves toward Janus Towers.[^19]

### Intel

- **[Confirmed]** Twenty-four entries—12 audio logs plus documents and artifacts—cover Richtofen's cloning studies, mansion/cult history, S.A.M.'s emergence, Janus research and the Serpent Mound.[^16]
- **[Confirmed]** “Deed of Sale” and “A Monstrous Proposal” are side-quest-gated files, demonstrating that optional secrets contain material lore rather than only rewards.[^16]

### Ciphers

- **[Confirmed]** Variant canisters, Doppelghast essence, spectral mirrors and ritual symbols form the quest's applied puzzle system. Mainframe prompts provide machine-verifiable solutions.

### Characters

- **[Confirmed]** The four-person crew is playable. S.A.M. becomes the betrayer; Richtofen becomes a temporary ally. Panos and Blanchard's pursuit runs through the broader Janus crisis.

### Confirmed Lore

- **[Confirmed]** S.A.M. is a synthetic person with Samantha's memories and an independent survival/body agenda; the real Maxis cannot simply be extracted as promised.[^20]
- **[Confirmed]** Weaver bears responsibility for the operation that accidentally killed Mary and Samuel, explaining Richtofen's personal vendetta.

### Theories

- **[Theory]** Whether a reconstructed body carrying Samantha's memories would count as “Samantha” is a philosophical identity question, not a settled technical fact.

## Reckoning

### Easter Egg

- **[Confirmed]** The finale assaults the three Janus Towers, stabilizes Aether Reactors, restarts the accelerator and obtains the Gorgofex. The crew prepares the Sentinel Artifact for one of two attunements.[^21][^22]
- **[Confirmed]** The decisive branch either replaces Samantha's sample with Mary and Samuel's DNA and fights S.A.M. for Richtofen, or allows S.A.M.'s body ritual and fights Richtofen. The Artifact completes the selected reconstruction.[^21][^23]
- **[Confirmed]** Regardless of branch, Ex-Requiem is transported into the Dark Aether and meets alternate versions of Richtofen, Dempsey, Takeo and Nikolai, setting up Black Ops 7.[^24]

### Cutscenes

- **[Confirmed]** In Richtofen's ending, S.A.M. is defeated and the Artifact restores Mary and Samuel. In S.A.M.'s ending, Richtofen is defeated and the system creates a living body modelled on Samantha for S.A.M.[^21][^24]
- **[Confirmed]** Both versions converge on the same final arrival: the crew falls into a Dark Aether plaza where the alternate four greet them and a new threat waits.

### Intel

- **[Confirmed]** Reckoning entries document Janus's cloning, the competing body protocols, S.A.M.'s use of Forsaken-derived abilities, Richtofen's family objective, Panos and Blanchard, and instability in the Towers.
- **[Confirmed]** The finale completes the game-wide total of 140 collectible Intel entries.[^3]

### Ciphers

- **[Confirmed]** Reactor stabilization, DNA sample selection, Artifact attunement and Gorgofex biomatter mechanisms form the operative puzzle chain. The final “answer” is intentionally a moral branch, not a hidden unique code.

### Characters

- **[Confirmed]** Ex-Requiem/Maya is playable; Richtofen and S.A.M. are alternative final bosses. Strauss, Peck, Ravenov and Raptor One provide support; Panos and Blanchard remain active around the facility.

### Confirmed Lore

- **[Confirmed]** The official Directed Mode description preserves both player options: restore Richtofen's family or help S.A.M. gain a body.[^23]
- **[Confirmed]** Black Ops 7 begins from the shared Dark Aether outcome but, as of 15 September 2026, has not unambiguously declared which reconstruction branch occurred. Community claims of a definitive canon choice conflict.[^25]

### Theories

- **[Theory]** Some players argue the Richtofen branch fits Black Ops 7's villain structure; others propose both branches became possible realities. Until an in-game Intel entry identifies the restored party, both are theories.[^25]

## Black Ops 6 synthesis

- **[Confirmed]** The season changes its central question from “Can Samantha be rescued?” to “Who owns Samantha's memories, and what can the Artifact make from them?”
- **[Confirmed]** Terminus and Liberty Falls are simultaneous halves of Janus's collapse; the Avalon maps recover the Artifact; Shattered Veil exposes S.A.M.; Reckoning forces the final attunement.
- **[Confirmed]** Chaos terminology—Sentinel Artifacts and Prima Materia—now exists inside Dark Aether continuity, but the exact continuity relationship remains deliberately incomplete.
- **[Theory]** The dual ending should be stored in the archive as two branches feeding one confirmed convergence, not flattened into an unsupported “canon” selection.
