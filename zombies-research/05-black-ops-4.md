# Black Ops 4 Zombies

**Eras:** Chaos story (Voyage of Despair, IX, Ancient Evil) and Aether story (Blood of the Dead, Classified, Alpha Omega, Tag der Toten)

Black Ops 4 runs two continuities in parallel. Chaos follows Scarlett Rhodes's search for her father through Sentinel trials and mythic sites. Aether begins when the Mob of the Dead cycle has been broken, derailing Richtofen's insurance plan; Nikolai then replaces him as leader and ends the multiverse rather than preserving another loop. The stories do not cross. **[Confirmed]**[^1][^2]

The supplied map list omits *Dead of the Night*. That map is chronologically the Chaos prologue—Alistair Rhodes's abduction and the formation of the rescue mission—but it is not expanded as a full map section here because it was not in scope. **[Confirmed]**[^3]

## IX

### Easter Egg

- **[Confirmed]** “Venerated Warrior” has the Chaos crew unlock Pack-a-Punch by defeating the four tower champions, prepare Death of Orion and complete trials for Danu, Ra, Zeus and Odin.[^4]
- **[Confirmed]** The summarized route purifies corrupted material in Danu's tower, lights four bull symbols and sacrifices the summoned Gladiators to Ra, kills special enemies in the order shown on Ra's obelisk, energizes Zeus lightning rods and survives Odin's subterranean trial. The crew then enters the arena portal and defeats two war elephants, Fury and Wrath.[^4]

### Cutscenes

- **[Confirmed]** The intro shows Scarlett, Diego, Bruno and Shaw inhaling vapour during their search, then awakening as gladiators in a Roman-Egyptian arena. The vision/trial occurs in 80 BCE while their physical bodies remain in the modern journey.
- **[Confirmed]** After the elephants fall, the High Priest points Scarlett toward Delphi. Back in the present, the crew awakens and finds Bruno apparently dead; Scarlett revives him with the Scepter of Ra, unaware that the Order's corruption now controls him.

### Intel

- **[Confirmed]** Challenge banners, inscriptions, the High Priest and the Oracle's voice establish that the crew is being tested through four divine cults. The Order has weaponized Sentinel rituals and Prima Materia rather than causing a 115 outbreak.
- **[Confirmed]** Character visions expose guilt and secrets, especially Diego's violence, Shaw's addiction, Bruno's criminal past and Scarlett's fixation on Alistair.

### Ciphers

- **[Confirmed]** IX contains god-temple messages and several short ciphertexts. Their solutions warn the crew about betrayal, descent and the cost of the trial; many are deliberately too short for unique cryptanalytic certainty.
- **[Confirmed]** In September 2026, one reported Bifid solution produced “You have no idea of just how far you have yet to fall”; another four-square solution produced “Your betrayal will not be forgiven.” The latter is below normal unicity distance, so the matching asset hash/padding is corroboration rather than mathematical uniqueness.[^5][^6]
- **[Theory]** Short AI-assisted solutions should remain marked “reported/reproducible” until independent re-encoding and asset context converge. A plausible English sentence alone is insufficient.

### Characters

- **[Confirmed]** Scarlett Rhodes, Diego Necalli, Bruno Delacroix and Stanton Shaw form the Chaos crew. The High Priest administers the trial; the Oracle/Medusa guides events remotely.

### Confirmed Lore

- **[Confirmed]** The trial points the crew toward Delphi and advances the Order's manipulation of Bruno. The ancient arena is a vision made physically lethal through the Sentinel Artifact.

### Theories

- **[Theory]** The exact metaphysics of whether the crew's full bodies travel to 80 BCE or occupy ritual avatars is not explained mechanically; “trial-vision with real consequences” preserves what is directly shown.

## Voyage of Despair

### Easter Egg

- **[Confirmed]** “Abandon Ship” begins when the crew activates the Sentinel Artifact on Titanic, opens its portals and unlocks Pack-a-Punch at four pedestals.[^7]
- **[Confirmed]** The summarized trial aligns four clocks and elemental outlets, kills Catalysts in matching ritual circles, decodes the Engine Room planets/constellations and shoots the planets in prescribed order, then completes timed elemental lockdowns.[^7]
- **[Confirmed]** The finale is fought against the Eye of Odin across successive decks as Titanic sinks. The crew weakens it with the Kraken and cleanses the Artifact.

### Cutscenes

- **[Confirmed]** The intro shows the crew boarding Titanic to steal the Artifact for information about Alistair. An Order agent activates it during the iceberg collision, transforming passengers and trapping the thieves in the trial.
- **[Confirmed]** In the ending, the crew survives on an ice floe with the cleansed Artifact. Scarlett receives the location Delphi, making the disaster a costly clue rather than a rescue.

### Intel

- **[Confirmed]** Phonographs describe previous seekers, the Artifact's trial and moral scrutiny. They repeatedly warn that possession is not proof of worthiness.[^8]
- **[Confirmed]** The ship's historical spaces are layered with Order cargo, ritual circles and Prima Materia transformations; the undead event is therefore attached to the heist, not presented as Titanic's ordinary history.

### Ciphers

- **[Confirmed]** Eleven character ciphers and twelve alchemical inscriptions are catalogued. Methods include semaphore, triliteral, QR, Elder Futhark, Atbash, scytale, vibration-encoded Braille, Morse, Welsh, fractionated Morse and maritime flags.[^9]
- **[Confirmed]** The character texts accuse each crew member of specific guilt. The alchemical set describes seven gates, transformation, unity of self/all, the Sentinel and the beast within.[^9]

### Characters

- **[Confirmed]** The four Chaos protagonists are playable. Alistair is the absent objective; Order agents and Odin's Eye oppose the trial.

### Confirmed Lore

- **[Confirmed]** Voyage occurs aboard RMS Titanic on 14 April 1912 and is chronologically after Dead of the Night but before IX.[^10]
- **[Confirmed]** Cleansing the Artifact provides the direction needed to reach Delphi.

### Theories

- **[Theory]** The Eye's exact identity is not fully defined. Its name and symbolism connect it to Odin, but treating it as the literal biological eye of the Norse deity goes beyond the text.

## Blood of the Dead

### Easter Egg

- **[Confirmed]** “Most Escape Alive” starts when Primis's planned Alcatraz stop fails. The crew restores both power systems, builds and charges the Spectral Shield, opens Pack-a-Punch, obtains the Hell's Retriever and reaches Richtofen's laboratory.[^11]
- **[Confirmed]** The summarized route follows a spectral bird through repeated shield visions, obtains the Kronorium, decodes its changing numbers, completes five prison challenges, frees ghostly allies, survives the Warden's capture and fights him at the Dark Mechanism.[^11]
- **[Confirmed]** Primis Richtofen enters the Dark Mechanism and is drained. Post-Revelations/Great War Richtofen leaves a cryopod, uses the Fire staff crystal to destroy the Warden, takes the Summoning Key and blood vials, and leaves the dying Richtofen behind. Nikolai assumes command.

### Cutscenes

- **[Confirmed]** The intro shows Primis reaching the Alcatraz laboratory for their normal blood-vial stop, discovering the Kronorium has changed and losing their portal when Brutus attacks.[^12]
- **[Confirmed]** The ending is the emotional break in the cycle: the crew refuses the old leader's pleas, follows a different Richtofen, and learns that continued dependence on the blood will perpetuate the loop.

### Intel

- **[Confirmed]** Radios and reels explain the Warden's devotion to the Shadowman, Richtofen's secret lab, Victis in cryostasis and the blood's theoretical use as an escape anchor.[^13]
- **[Confirmed]** Spectral visions and the prisoners' ghosts show that Arlington's cycle-breaking victory weakened the pocket dimension's established order.

### Ciphers

- **[Confirmed]** Fourteen major texts use ADFGVX and related systems, many with the key **BLOODOFTHEDEAD**, plus four scraps forming a Playfair text.[^14]
- **[Confirmed]** Much of the plaintext reconstructs letters from Sal, Billy and Finn, humanizing the prisoners and documenting regrets absent from Mob's original cycle account.[^14]

### Characters

- **[Confirmed]** Primis is playable. The Warden/Brutus serves the Shadowman; Weasel and other Mob spirits assist; Post-Revelations Richtofen replaces the dying cycle-Richtofen.

### Confirmed Lore

- **[Confirmed]** The official trailer states that the Kronorium's predicted future is now in jeopardy, and the map confirms that the cycle has changed.[^12]
- **[Confirmed]** Leadership transfers to Nikolai because his soul remains in his body, allowing him to read the Kronorium differently from the others.

### Theories

- **[Theory]** Calling the arriving Richtofen simply “future Richtofen” hides a complicated loop. “Post-Revelations/Great War Richtofen” is the more precise provenance.

## Classified

### Easter Egg

- **[Confirmed]** “Cold War Remedy” is a substantial side quest to reactivate Project Skadi and obtain the Winter's Howl: recover four numeric codes from photographs and portraits, use them at the War Room display, route teleporter and DEFCON systems, and complete the weapon assembly.[^15]
- **[Confirmed]** Classified's narrative ending is not attached to a normal multi-step boss quest. Reaching round 150 triggers the canonical cinematic.

### Cutscenes

- **[Confirmed]** The intro depicts Ultimis accidentally arriving at the Pentagon in 1963, overlapping the crisis associated with *Five* and fragmenting the group through teleportation.
- **[Confirmed]** The ending jumps forward to show Primis and Ultimis together in confinement after Moon. Primis Nikolai declares that they will fight the Great War, while Samantha insists that the real target is Monty.

### Intel

- **[Confirmed]** Reels, punched cards, phones and wisps greatly expand Operation Stapler, the Pentagon's study of Richtofen's materials, McNamara's containment problems, Samantha and the Ultimis post-Moon transfer.
- **[Confirmed]** The map retroactively bridges *Five*, Groom Lake and Hangar 4 without replacing the older map's events.

### Ciphers

- **[Confirmed]** Five known ciphers are solved. An Übchi message keyed **CLASSIFIED** records Richtofen's eccentric Operation Stapler demands, while other files document the Pentagon's recovered technology and institutional response.[^16]
- **[Confirmed]** Project Skadi's photograph numbers are an operational code puzzle, distinct from the optional ciphertext archive.[^15]

### Characters

- **[Confirmed]** Ultimis is the playable crew. The Pentagon cast, Cornelius Pernell and American officials appear through recordings/history; Primis appears in the ending.

### Confirmed Lore

- **[Confirmed]** After Moon, Ultimis is recovered and ultimately held at Groom Lake, enabling the Primis–Ultimis alliance seen in Alpha Omega.

### Theories

- **[Theory]** The round-150 requirement is a gameplay gate, not evidence that the characters canonically fought exactly 150 rounds before the rescue.

## Ancient Evil

### Easter Egg

- **[Confirmed]** “Greek Tragedy” has the Chaos crew activate the Delphi Artifact, free Pegasus, open Pack-a-Punch and redeem/exalt the four Hands of Gaia, Charon, Ouranos and Hemera.[^17]
- **[Confirmed]** The summarized trial ignites oil with Apollo's Will, solves deity-specific offering and symbol tests, enters the amphitheatre, survives Sparta and river sequences, and defeats Perseus with the exalted Hands before freeing the Oracle.[^17]

### Cutscenes

- **[Confirmed]** The intro shows the crew entering hidden Delphi, where the apparently bound Oracle asks them to release her and claims Alistair is also captive.
- **[Confirmed]** The ending reveals the deception: the Oracle is Medusa. She drains Scarlett's knowledge, shows Alistair petrified, and orders the enthralled Bruno and Shaw to secure the library while Diego escapes with an unconscious Scarlett.[^18]

### Intel

- **[Confirmed]** Eight Perseus scrolls chronicle his coercion of the Pythia, imprisonment of her sisters, year-long failure of the trial and transformation into the undead warlord.[^19]
- **[Confirmed]** Oracle Tales describe Delphi, Apollo, Python and the city's fall, but their narrator is a manipulator; mythic history and Medusa's self-serving account must be distinguished.[^20]

### Ciphers

- **[Confirmed]** Greek inscriptions, constellation/deity symbols and the Hand trials encode operational solutions. Unlike Voyage's broad cipher gallery, Ancient Evil's principal written archive is delivered as voiced scrolls and Oracle tales.
- **[Theory]** The Oracle's mythological claims cannot all be treated as neutral history after the ending exposes her identity and agenda.

### Characters

- **[Confirmed]** The Chaos crew is playable. Medusa poses as the Oracle; Perseus is the undead warlord; Alistair is shown petrified.

### Confirmed Lore

- **[Confirmed]** The Chaos season ends unresolved: Medusa is released, the crew is divided and the library is threatened. No later Black Ops 4 map resolves that cliffhanger.

### Theories

- **[Theory]** Planned continuation details inferred from unused material or later interviews should not be presented as released canon.

## Alpha Omega

### Easter Egg

- **[Confirmed]** “Electromagnetic Awakening Party” begins Nikolai's plan. The combined crews restore Camp Edward, enter timed punch-clock codes, satisfy Rushmore's Broken Arrow tests, build elemental Ray Gun Mark IIs, assemble an elemental orb and open the American Pyramid Device.[^21]
- **[Confirmed]** The finale draws the Avogadro out of the APD, weakens it with the facility systems and transports it to the Hanford site—causally setting up the electrical entity encountered in TranZit.[^22]

### Cutscenes

- **[Confirmed]** The intro shows Primis and Ultimis entering a seemingly quiet Nuketown/Camp Edward to obtain the Elemental Shard.
- **[Confirmed]** The ending reunites Samantha and Eddie after Maxis is killed at the House by Monty. Samantha destroys the facility in rage and promises to fight; Nikolai states that one final journey remains.

### Intel

- **[Confirmed]** Three audio reels, five film reels, five punch cards, three players and five radios document Broken Arrow, Pernell, the Elemental Shard, the APD, the Avogadro and American experimentation.[^23]
- **[Confirmed]** Documents provide Rushmore directory codes and dated memoranda, including institutional reaction to the Pentagon incident and the history of Camp Edward projects.[^24]

### Ciphers

- **[Confirmed]** Rushmore's four-digit directory is an explicit searchable code system. Punch clocks, television codes and elemental symbols form the quest's main decoding layer.[^21][^24]
- **[Confirmed]** The archive's most important result is causal: Cornelius Pernell becomes the Avogadro, and the quest sends him to the location where Victis will encounter him later.

### Characters

- **[Confirmed]** Primis and Ultimis are both present, with playable selection across eight characters. Rushmore assists; Pernell/Avogadro is the antagonist; Samantha and Eddie arrive at the end.

### Confirmed Lore

- **[Confirmed]** The Elemental Shard is a self-replenishing concentration of 115-derived power and a required component of Nikolai's endgame.[^23]
- **[Confirmed]** Alpha Omega links the pre-TranZit Avogadro history to the post-Revelations crew's final operation.

### Theories

- **[Theory]** The facility's nuclear detonation is not the sole cause of its visible destruction; the ending and map evidence place Samantha's outburst at the decisive moment.[^22]

## Tag der Toten

### Easter Egg

- **[Confirmed]** “Salvation Lies Above” sends Victis to the Siberian facility to construct the Agarthan Device for Nikolai. The crew completes Hermit's challenges, repairs the lighthouse systems, obtains the Seal of Duality and combines the Apothicon Blood with the Elemental Shard.[^25]
- **[Confirmed]** The summarized finale escorts and charges the device through the facility as reality collapses, carries it to the ship and survives the last lockdown before handing the completed mechanism to Nikolai.[^25]
- **[Confirmed]** A final playable epilogue has Samantha and Eddie walk from the Dark Aether into a new universe while the dead crews' hopes for ordinary lives are heard.[^25]

### Cutscenes

- **[Confirmed]** The intro shows Victis released from Alcatraz cryostasis and recruited by Richtofen acting for Nikolai.
- **[Confirmed]** The ending takes place around the forest camp: Primis and Ultimis have been poisoned. Nikolai explains that they and the Aether must die, has Samantha shoot him, and destroys the Summoning Key with the Device. The multiverse, 115, Keepers, Apothicons and the crews are banished into the Dark Aether; Samantha and Eddie alone enter the clean universe.

### Intel

- **[Confirmed]** Reels, radios and phonographs follow Pablo Marinus, Richtofen, Yena, Gersh, the Hermit and Siberian experiments. Pablo's record explains how Great War Richtofen left the cycle to reach Alcatraz.[^26]
- **[Confirmed]** The archive resolves long-running threads around the frozen facility, Golden Rod, Ultimis specimens and blood-vial insurance rather than merely revisiting Call of the Dead scenery.

### Ciphers

- **[Confirmed]** The Seal, lighthouse lenses, dials and elemental charge order form the quest's central symbolic/mechanical puzzle. The map emphasizes audio history more than a new large classical-cipher collection.
- **[Confirmed]** “Salvation Lies Above” and “Ascend from Darkness” intentionally reuse Nacht wall phrases, closing the Aether story with language from its first map.[^25]

### Characters

- **[Confirmed]** Victis is playable. Primis, Ultimis, Samantha and Eddie appear in the camp ending; Nikolai commands the operation; Pablo and the Hermit guide its history.

### Confirmed Lore

- **[Confirmed]** Nikolai's solution is annihilation of the corrupted multiverse, not another repair. The old universes are sent to the Dark Aether and a singular uncorrupted universe remains.[^25]
- **[Confirmed]** Samantha Maxis and the child Eddie survive into that new reality, creating the human foundation for the Dark Aether story.

### Theories

- **[Theory]** The heroes' campfire wishes describe desired lives, not confirmed biographies in the new universe.
- **[Theory]** “Banished to the Dark Aether” does not mean every old being was permanently destroyed; later Dark Aether entries show that remnants and memories can persist.

## Black Ops 4 synthesis

- **[Confirmed]** Chaos ends on a deliberate cliffhanger with Medusa ascendant; Aether reaches a definitive cosmological transition.
- **[Confirmed]** Blood breaks the repeating Great War route, Alpha Omega gathers the Shard and Tag uses it to collapse the corrupted multiverse.
- **[Confirmed]** The season's archive is unusually multimodal: phonographs, spectral visions, punch cards, algorithmic ciphers, spoken myths, computer directories and ending films all carry essential facts.
- **[Theory]** Calling Tag an erasure of all prior meaning confuses destruction of the multiverse with narrative invalidation. The Dark Aether saga explicitly inherits Samantha, Eddie and buried consequences.
